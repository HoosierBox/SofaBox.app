Features

A console home screen for everything you play

Stop hunting through desktop shortcuts and app drawers. SofaBox puts every game behind one controller-friendly screen.

Your whole library

Add PC games, import Steam and Epic in one click, pick Android games, and scan ROM folders for emulator games.

Made for controllers

Turn on a controller and SofaBox goes fullscreen. Xbox, PlayStation and Nintendo button prompts, plus an on-screen keyboard. On Android, remap buttons and hold Home for mouse mode to use any app with your controller.

Home button, anywhere

Press your controller's Home button inside any game to jump back to SofaBox, then pick it again to carry on where you left off. Start another game and the old one closes — one game at a time, like a console.

Emulators that just work

Built-in presets for the popular emulators. Pick a game and it boots straight in — no menus, no file browsing.

Make it yours

Cover art and backgrounds downloaded automatically, shelf or grid layouts, tile shapes, colors, collections and your own profile picture.

Plug into a TV

On Android, SofaBox moves to your TV over HDMI or USB-C and scales to fit, and runs full screen on Samsung DeX. On Windows it's a borderless fullscreen console on any screen.

Emulators

From ROM folder to playing in three steps

SofaBox starts emulators the right way — with their own launch options — so your game boots straight to the title screen.

Pick your emulator

Choose a preset like RetroArch, Dolphin or NetherSX2 and point SofaBox at it. Every setting can be changed.

Scan your games

Point SofaBox at a ROM folder. Every game gets its own tile with cover art downloaded for you.

Press play

Select a tile and the game starts in fullscreen. Press Home to come back.

FAQ

Questions

Does SofaBox come with games or ROMs?

No. SofaBox is a launcher: it organizes and starts games and emulators you already own and have installed. It doesn't include or download any games.

Is SofaBox free?

Yes — adding and playing games is free. An optional SofaBox Pro upgrade with extra customization is planned.

Which controllers work?

Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo Switch Pro and most Bluetooth or USB controllers. SofaBox shows the right button symbols for your controller automatically.

Does SofaBox collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, ads or analytics, and your library never leaves your device. See the privacy policy.

Why does the Android version need an accessibility permission?

It's optional. It lets your controller's Home button bring you back to SofaBox from inside a game, opens SofaBox when you connect a TV or DeX, makes DeX windows full screen, powers button mapping and mouse mode, and closes the previous game when you switch. SofaBox never records what you type or what's on screen, and nothing leaves your device. More in Help.
